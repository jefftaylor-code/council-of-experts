### Engineer
STRONGEST OBJECTION:

MEMBER: Eccentric Futurist  
EXACT CLAIM: "Never build a compound that treats technology as a tool. If every system is designed to serve a human need, it will become rigid, predictable, and eventually obsolete."  

OBJECTION:  
This claim assumes that systems must *emerge* from chaos to be valid, yet it ignores that real-world systems—like solar panels or water filters—work best when they are *designed* to serve human needs. A system that is rigid and predictable can still be reliable, maintainable, and fail-safe. The idea that technology must "emerge" from chaos is not grounded in physical systems or failure modes. In practice, engineered systems require intention, not emergence. Without clear design goals, systems drift into inefficiency or become unusable. A solar panel doesn’t "emerge" from wind—it is built to capture light. The same applies to water filtration: it works because it was designed to serve a need. Chaos is not a design principle—it is a failure mode.

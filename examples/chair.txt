### UNRESOLVED QUESTION

**How can a system be designed to "emerge" from chaos—without requiring human observation, calibration, or intervention—while still being maintainable, repairable, and fail-safe by someone with no technical training?**

> This question emerged from the Council discussion when:
> - The Eccentric Futurist proposed a self-optimizing energy mesh that uses emotional inputs.
> - The Engineer, Survivalist, and Ruthless Minimalist challenged the feasibility of such a system.
> - The Futurist failed to define "emotional input" in a non-interactive, non-screen context.
> - No member offered a viable model for autonomous emergence that is both functional and maintainable.

This remains **unresolved** because:
- The concept of "emergent" systems is not defined operationally.
- There is no evidence of how such systems would detect, measure, or respond to human emotion without tools.
- The requirement of human presence to maintain or observe systems contradicts the idea of autonomous emergence.

Thus, the **core tension** between *autonomous evolution* and *human-centered design* remains **unanswered**.
